## The app

Acatability is a journal you keep on your own or in a **pact** with up to
four friends. You write privately. The others in your pact see only what
the [Privacy Policy](privacy) says they can see.

## Your account

- You sign in with Apple. Keep your Apple ID secure. Anything done in the

- You must be at least 13 years old.
- You can delete your account at any time in **Settings → Delete

## Free on your own

Using Acatability on your own is **free, for as long as Acatability is
offered**: checking in, your whole archive, search and "your days". You
never need to pay to keep a journal.

## Pacts

- A pact is up to **5 people**: the person who pays and up to 4 people
  they invite.
- **The person who pays is the only one who can invite.** Invited members
  don't pay and can't invite.
- Shared features are: seeing each other's check-ins and what each person
  chooses to share, "days together", and inviting people.
- **If the person paying for a pact stops paying, or leaves the pact or
  deletes their account, shared features pause for everyone in it until
  someone in the pact subscribes.** That person then becomes the payer and
  can invite. Nobody's journal, entries, "your days" or "days together"
  are deleted while a pact is paused. They show again when it restarts.
- If you subscribe while you're in a pact that someone else is paying for,
  the pact stays theirs. Your subscription takes over the pact only if it
  pauses. We can't cancel a subscription for you; see below for how to.
- The person who pays can remove someone from the pact. Anyone can leave
  a pact at any time in **Settings → Leave pact**. Leaving or being
  removed never deletes your own entries.

## Free trial

- Every new account gets a **14-day free trial**. It starts when you
  **create your account**, not when you buy anything. There is one trial
  per account.
- During the trial you can start a pact of up to 5 and invite people, the
  same as a subscriber.
- You don't need to enter payment details for the trial, and it doesn't
  turn into a paid subscription on its own.
- When the trial ends without a purchase, any pact you started pauses (see
  **Pacts**). Everything you do on your own stays free.

## Subscription

- Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
- **It renews automatically at the end of each period (each month or each
  year)** unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your
  Apple ID is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the
  period ends.
- Switching between monthly and yearly takes effect **at your next
  renewal**.
- **To cancel**, go to iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions →
  Acatability**, and turn off renewal. You keep access until the end of
  the period you've paid for.
- Deleting the app or your account does **not** cancel the subscription.
  Cancel it in Settings as above.
- If we ever change the price, Apple will tell you first, and ask for your
  agreement where the law requires it.
