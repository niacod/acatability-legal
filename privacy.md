# Acatability Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Acatability is a journaling app you can keep on your own or share in a
pact with up to four friends. It is made and
run by Qurek Limited, a company registered in England and Wales ("we",
"us"). This
page explains what the app stores, where it's kept, who can see it, and how
to delete it.

If you have a question, or want to use any of the rights below, email
**info@qurek.biz**.

## The short version

- We collect only what the app needs to work. There is no advertising, no
  tracking, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
- Your journal text is private. Nobody in your pact ever sees it.
- The others in your pact see only your name, your cat, which days you
  checked in, and whatever you choose to share on each entry.
- If you pay, we keep a record of your purchase that we get from Apple.
  Nobody else in your pact sees it.
- Notifications are off until you say yes. If you do, a friend's
  notification only ever says that you checked in, never what you wrote.
- You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at
  any time.

## What we collect

| Data | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | The name Apple or Google gives us when you sign in. If you sign in with Apple and don't share it, we don't have it. | So the others in your pact know who you are |
| **Email address** | The email Apple or Google gives us. If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", this is a private relay address from Apple, not your real one. | Your account login |
| **User ID** | A random ID that we create for your account, plus the ID that Sign in with Apple or Google uses | To tell your data apart from everyone else's |
| **Journal entries** | For each day: your journal text, your gratitude items, journal and meditation minutes, and your sharing choices for that day | This is the app |
| **Settings** | Cat colour, journaling goal, sharing preference, preferred journaling time, and your device's time zone | To set up the app for you and to know when your day starts and ends |
| **Pact membership** | Which pact you're in, who set it up, when you joined, and any invite codes you create or use | To connect you with the others in your pact, and to know who can invite |
| **Purchase history** | If you buy a subscription or the Founding Pact: the purchase's transaction ID, its type (monthly, yearly or Founding Pact), its status (for example active, expired or refunded), when it expires or was refunded, and whether it was a test purchase. We get this from Apple, never from your card. | To know whether your pact's shared features are on, and how many people you can invite |
| **Push token** | If you allow notifications: a code Apple gives your phone so we can send it a notification, and whether it's a test build | To tell you when a friend in your pact checks in |
| **Friend notification settings and record** | Whether "When a friend checks in" is on, and on which days you sent or were sent a friend notification | So you can turn them off, and so nobody gets more than one a day |
| **Apple sign-in token** | A token from Apple, kept only on our server and never readable by the app | So we can cancel your Sign in with Apple link when you delete your account, as Apple requires |

We use all of this only to run the app. None of it is used for tracking or
advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers.

Your data is linked to your account, because a journal that isn't linked
to you would be of no use to you.

## What stays on your phone

Drafts you haven't saved yet are kept only on your phone until they're
uploaded. Your login session is stored in the iOS Keychain. Your
reminder settings (on or off, and what time) and the reminders your cat
has scheduled are kept only on your phone. So are whether memories are
on, which recaps you've opened, and what your cat has already said to
you. We never see them.

## Where it's stored

Your account and entries are stored with **Supabase**, our database
provider, on servers in **London, United Kingdom (AWS eu-west-2)**. Data is
encrypted when it's sent to and from the app, and access is locked down so
that each account can read only what it is allowed to read.

Sign-in is handled by **Apple** (Sign in with Apple) or **Google** (Sign in
with Google), whichever you choose. Google gives us only your name, email
address and Google account ID. If you buy anything,
payment is handled by **Apple** through the App Store. We never see your
card details. When you buy, the app gives Apple your random account ID so
the purchase is tied to your Acatability account, and our server then asks
Apple for the purchase's details. Apple also tells our server when a
subscription renews, ends or is refunded.

If you allow notifications, friend notifications are delivered by
**Apple** (Apple Push Notification service). Apple receives your phone's
push token and the notification's words: your cat's name and your
friend's name. Daily reminders never leave your phone.

Supabase, Apple and (if you sign in with Google) Google process data for us. We don't share your data with
anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

## Who can see what

- **You** can see everything you've written, and your own "your days"
  count. Memories ("One month ago today, you wrote…") and recaps are shown
  only to you.
- **Everyone else in your pact** (up to four people) can see:
  - your name and your cat's colour;
  - whether you checked in on a given day, and the pact's "days
    together";
  - your gratitude items for a day, **only** if you turned sharing on for
    that entry;
  - your journal and meditation minutes for a day, **only** if you turned
    sharing on for that entry.

  They see what you shared for a day only after they've checked in on
  that same day too.
- **Your journal text is never shared** with anyone in your pact or with
  anyone else. The database keeps it apart from the shared data, and
  nothing ever copies it across.
- **Your recap stays on your phone** unless you share it. Your cat's
  recap is worked out on your phone from your own gratitude items. If you
  tap Share, the app makes a picture with your counts and up to three
  favourites (never your journal text or the "picked for you" items), and your phone's share menu sends it wherever you choose. It
  doesn't go through our servers.
- **Your purchase history is never shown to anyone else in your pact.**
  They don't see your price, your plan or its dates. They know who
  invited them, and if the person who pays stops paying, everyone in the
  pact sees that shared features have paused. Nothing more.
- **We** (Qurek Limited) can technically reach the database to run and fix
  the service. We don't read your entries.

**Friend notifications.** When you check in, others in your pact who
allowed notifications may get one saying so, for example "Meow! Maya just
checked in. Want to join?". That's the one way someone can learn you've
checked in before they have themselves. It names you and nothing else:
never your journal text, gratitude items, minutes or anything you shared.
Nobody gets one before 8am or after 9pm in their own time zone, or more
than once a day, or after they've checked in themselves.

If you turn sharing off for an entry, or delete it, the others stop
seeing it. If you leave a pact, or the person who pays removes you, the
others stop seeing everything you had shared with it.

## Notifications

Acatability doesn't send notifications until you say yes. There are two
kinds:

- **Daily reminders from your cat.** Your phone schedules these itself,
  only on days you haven't checked in, and they stop if you're away for
  a while. Nothing about them is sent to us.
- **Friend notifications**, when someone in your pact checks in (see
  "Who can see what" above).

Turn either off in the app under **Settings → Reminders**, or turn all of
them off in **iPhone Settings → Notifications → Acatability**. Neither
kind is used for marketing.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Unused
invite codes expire after 7 days. A push token is deleted when you sign
out, when someone else signs in on that phone, or when Apple tells us
it no longer works (for example after you delete the app). Purchase records are kept for as long as
your account exists, because a subscription or Founding Pact can still
count after a long gap.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**. This immediately and
permanently deletes:

- your account and profile;
- every journal entry;
- everything you had shared with your pact;
- your place in your pact. The others keep their own entries. If only one
  person would be left, the pact ends. If you were paying, the pact
  pauses until someone else in it subscribes;
- your invite codes;
- your push tokens and friend notification record;
- your purchase records (this doesn't cancel an Apple subscription;
  cancel that in iPhone Settings);
- the Apple sign-in token, after we ask Apple to cancel the link to your
  Apple ID.

It can't be undone. Copies in our database provider's backups are replaced
automatically within 30 days.

You can also email **info@qurek.biz** and ask us to delete your account
for you.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to ask for a
copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to
how we use it, and to take it elsewhere. Email **info@qurek.biz** and we
will answer within one month.

We rely on **performing our contract with you** (providing the app you
signed up for) as the legal basis for using your data.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to
the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

Acatability is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect data from them. If you think a child has made an account, email us
and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change
matters, such as collecting something new, we'll tell you in the app
before it takes effect.
